# Argus Research Company (argus-investor)

> Argus Research Company is an independent equity research firm founded in New York in 1934,
> part of the Argus Research Group. It produces fundamental and quantitative Buy/Hold/Sell
> ratings, target prices, earnings estimates, model portfolios and economic commentary on
> US equities, and distributes them to institutional and brokerage clients and through
> third-party platforms. Argus is not a broker-dealer: it does not underwrite, broker trades,
> make markets or manage money on the research side.
